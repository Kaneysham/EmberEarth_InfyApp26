Our Product

Information About Our Digital Product

Pitch

Harvest Hope is an Irish potato famine historical game offering two distinct perspectives. As Mateo the farmer, you manage crops and quests, facing the mental burden of sustaining your community. As Albert the governor, you make macro-level political and financial choices, highlighting how government policies either save or break vulnerable communities.

Theme Implementation

Our game implements the theme of "Outgrow Hunger" through the time period. We based our game on a point in history where thousands of people lacked nutrition, we also highlight the real world factors that effect world nutrition.

Why We Built This

Ember Earth wishes to spread awareness about this issue through our game. We’ll use emotion to provoke empathy in our players, this will result in them truly understanding the problem and one day helping to solve it.
